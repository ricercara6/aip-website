AOTEA‑10T Technical Report

A sovereign foundation model for public administration

We describe AOTEA‑10T, a proposed 10T-parameter model trained on public-sector corpora, legislative history, anonymised service telemetry, and civic submissions. The system is designed for routing, summarisation, policy simulation, and auditable decision support.
