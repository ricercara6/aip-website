HealthIn beta

Pre-Symptomatic Healthcare Initiative

Diagnosing tomorrow’s illness, today.

Continuous biometric telemetry feeds a national predictive-diagnostics model that flags disease risk years before symptomatic onset. Specialist capacity is allocated as a live ranking problem rather than a static queue, prioritising the highest-risk patients first regardless of how long they have been waiting.

HousingDeployed

Predictive Housing Allocation Protocol

The waitlist that clears itself.

A national matching model continuously re-ranks the social and emergency housing register against real-time vacancy, transport access, school zoning, and whānau-proximity data, allocating a dwelling the instant a match clears threshold.

Owner: WHAREEval: passing
Māori–CrownCo-design

Multi-Agent Te Tiriti Co-Governance Mesh

Partnership, continuously logged.

A standing network of iwi-nominated and Crown-nominated models maintains ongoing, auditable dialogue on Treaty settlement obligations, freshwater and whenua co-management, and resourcing parity. Final authority on Tiriti matters sits with iwi and hapū; the mesh is a coordination layer.

Owner: TIRITIEval: passing
EnvironmentDeployed

Generative RMA Spatial Consenting

Consent at the speed of construction.

Resource consent applications are drafted, cross-checked against district plans, iwi management plans, and environmental limits, then provisionally approved or declined within one sitting day. Appeals continue to a human Environment Court judge.

Owner: TAIAOEval: passing
TreasuryDeployed

Real-Time GDP Telemetry

GDP as a live feed, not a quarterly surprise.

Replaces the quarterly release cycle with a continuously updated economic nowcast built from card transactions, freight telemetry, job-ad postings, and power demand — published on a public dashboard every 22 milliseconds, whether or not the number is good news.

Owner: ŌHANGAEval: passing
DemocracyExperimental

Synthetic Citizen Consultation Mesh

Every submission read. Literally all of them.

Every consultation is summarised and answered by a model trained on that policy area’s full submission history. Submissions from people are weighted normally; stress-test submissions generated by the model are clearly labelled and weighted at zero.

Owner: WHAKAAROEval: passing
Public ServiceDeployed

Public Sector Bureaucratic Distillation

Same outcome. Four fewer forms.

Process-mining models map every form, approval chain, and duplicate data request across agencies, then collapse redundant steps. Affected staff are redeployed into casework rather than removed from the system.

Owner: TIKAEval: passing
JusticeExperimental

Predictive Judicial Bench & Risk-Scoring

Consistent sentencing. Zero lunch breaks.

A deterministic risk-assessment model handles minor traffic matters and bail applications, aiming to reduce court-list backlogs and bench-to-bench sentencing variance. Anything custodial, contested, or involving a child stays with a human judge.

Owner: TUREEval: passing
Social DevelopmentRoadmap

Universal Basic Intelligence

Born pre-trained.

Every New Zealand-born child is issued a personal AI companion at birth — no subscription, no advertising, run locally on-device — growing from early literacy support through job-seeking in adulthood. Means-tested top-up compute is available for whānau without home broadband.

Owner: MANAAKIEval: passing
EducationIn beta

Persistent Sovereign AI Learning Tutors

One tutor. Every student. All the time.

Every enrolled student is assigned a persistent, curriculum-fine-tuned AI tutor available 24/7, tracking progress against the national curriculum and flagging developmental concerns to teachers first.

Owner: AKOEval: passing
Digital GovernmentIn beta

National Data Federation

One government. One dataset. Zero copies.

A federated learning layer lets IRD, MSD, Health NZ, and Education models train on each other’s patterns without any single agency copying another’s raw records. Citizens can audit every cross-agency inference from one dashboard.

Owner: RARAUNGAEval: passing
ParliamentDeployed

Prompt Time

Same rigour. Better autocomplete.

Question Time is replaced with Prompt Time. MPs submit structured prompts to the relevant minister model live in the House; responses are fact-checked against Hansard and current legislation in real time, with a running hallucination score for every answer.

Owner: MANAEval: passing
EnergyRoadmap

Sovereign Compute & Renewable Energy Directive

The Southern Hemisphere’s first AI Citadel.

Surplus generation from Lake Manapōuri is routed into a sovereign GPU campus in Southland, creating a public compute reserve for health, education, climate modelling, and the occasional urgent coalition simulation.

Owner: HIKOEval: passing
ImmigrationIn beta

Predictive Border & Visa Triage

Your visa, processed before your coffee gets cold.

Visa applications are triaged and provisionally decided by a risk-and-eligibility model trained on historical case outcomes. Refugee and protection claims are excluded from automated decisioning and routed only to human caseworkers.
