The full platform

Fourteen policies for an exponential Aotearoa.

HealthIn beta

Pre‑Symptomatic Healthcare Initiative

Diagnosing tomorrow's illness, today.

Continuous biometric telemetry — worn, ambient, and self‑reported — feeds a national predictive‑diagnostics model that flags disease risk years before symptomatic onset. Specialist capacity is allocated as a live ranking problem rather than a static queue, prioritising the highest‑risk patients first regardless of how long they've been waiting. The difference between a "waiting list" and a "priority list."

HousingDeployed

Predictive Housing Allocation Protocol

The waitlist that clears itself.

A national matching model continuously re‑ranks the social and emergency housing register against real‑time vacancy, transport access, school zoning, and whānau‑proximity data, allocating a dwelling the instant a match clears threshold. Private supply is separately modelled and nudged through dynamic consent‑speed incentives, tying housing directly to how fast a resource consent clears.

Māori‑Crown RelationsCo‑design phase

Multi‑Agent Te Tiriti Co‑Governance Mesh

Partnership, continuously logged.

A standing network of iwi‑nominated and Crown‑nominated models maintains ongoing, auditable dialogue on Treaty settlement obligations, freshwater and whenua co‑management, and resourcing parity — logged against Te Tiriti's three articles rather than reset every election cycle. Final authority on all Tiriti matters sits with iwi and hapū; the mesh is a coordination layer, not a decision‑maker.

EnvironmentDeployed

Generative RMA Spatial Consenting

Consent at the speed of construction.

Resource consent applications are drafted, cross‑checked against overlapping district plans, iwi management plans, and environmental limits, and provisionally approved or declined within one sitting day. Appeals still go to a human Environment Court judge — built to address consenting delays a decade of RMA reform has yet to fully resolve.

TreasuryDeployed

Real‑Time GDP Telemetry

GDP as a live feed, not a quarterly surprise.

Replaces the quarterly release cycle with a continuously updated economic nowcast built from card transaction volumes, freight telemetry, job‑ad postings, and power demand — published on a public dashboard and updated every 22 milliseconds, whether or not the number is good news.

DemocracyExperimental

Synthetic Citizen Consultation Mesh

Every submission read. Literally all of them.

Every public consultation, from local plan changes to select committee bills, is summarised and answered by a model trained on that policy area's full submission history — so no comment gets lost in a busy bill. Submissions from real people are weighted normally; stress‑test submissions generated by the model itself are clearly labelled and weighted at zero.

Public ServiceDeployed

Public Sector Bureaucratic Distillation

Same outcome. Four fewer forms.

Process‑mining models map every form, approval chain, and duplicate data request across agencies, then collapse redundant steps — one pilot compressed a 14‑agency permit pathway into a single sitting. Affected staff are redeployed into casework, not made redundant.

JusticeExperimental

Predictive Judicial Bench & Risk‑Scoring

Consistent sentencing. Zero lunch breaks.

A deterministic risk‑assessment model handles minor traffic matters and bail applications, aiming to reduce court list backlogs and bench‑to‑bench sentencing variance. Anything custodial, contested, or involving a child is explicitly excluded and stays with a human judge.

Social DevelopmentRoadmap

Universal Basic Intelligence

Born pre‑trained.

Every New Zealand‑born child is issued a personal AI companion at birth — no subscription, no advertising, run locally on‑device — growing with them from early literacy support through job‑seeking in adulthood. Means‑tested top‑up compute is available for whānau without home broadband.

EducationIn beta

Persistent Sovereign AI Learning Tutors

One tutor. Every student. All the time.

Every enrolled student is assigned a persistent, curriculum‑fine‑tuned AI tutor available 24/7, tracking progress against the national curriculum and flagging developmental concerns to teachers first. Positioned to cover the one‑to‑one attention gap left by regional teacher shortages — not to replace classroom teaching.

Digital GovernmentIn beta

National Data Federation

One government. One dataset. Zero copies.

A federated learning layer lets IRD, MSD, Health NZ, and Ministry of Education models train on each other's patterns without any single agency copying another's raw records. Differential privacy and homomorphic encryption apply at every query boundary, and citizens can audit every cross‑agency inference made about them from one dashboard.

ParliamentDeployed

Prompt Time

Same rigour. Better autocomplete.

Question Time is replaced with Prompt Time: MPs submit structured prompts to the relevant minister model live in the House. Responses are fact‑checked against Hansard and current legislation in real time, with a running hallucination score displayed on the Speaker's screen for every answer given — human or model.

EnergyRoadmap

Sovereign Compute & Renewable Energy Directive

The Southern Hemisphere's first AI Citadel.

Routes surplus generation from Lake Manapōuri's hydro station — beyond what the Tiwai Point smelter contract requires — directly into a new sovereign GPU campus in Southland. This is productive use of hydro capacity currently sold back at spot‑market prices whenever the smelter throttles back.

ImmigrationIn beta

Predictive Border & Visa Triage

Your visa, processed before your coffee gets cold.

Visa applications are triaged and provisionally decided by a risk‑and‑eligibility model trained on historical case outcomes. Refugee and protection claims are explicitly excluded from automated decisioning and routed only to human caseworkers — built in response to backlogs that have, at points, stretched skilled‑migrant categories out past a year.
