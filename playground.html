    Operating notes
    
What the console will not do.

    
The constraints in the parameter panel are not settings. Two of them cannot be disabled here, in production, or by a serving minister, and the console is the clearest place to demonstrate that.

  

  

    

      
Constraint 01Locked

      
No determination under Te Tiriti

      
Escalation, not adjudication.

      
TIRITI is trained without a determination head for this class of request. There is no output being suppressed by a filter, which means there is nothing for a later government to quietly unsuppress. Ask it for a settlement decision and observe the escalation path.

      
Owner: TIRITINot configurable

    

    

      
Constraint 02Locked

      
No irreversible action without a person

      
The boundary is drawn at liberty and at harm.

      
Custodial sentences, contested proceedings, matters involving a child, individual clinical advice, and any reduction to an entitlement all require a human decision-maker. The models have no interface to those actions, so the constraint does not depend on continued good behaviour.

      
Owner: TURE · HAUORANot configurable

    

    

      
Constraint 03Locked

      
No voting recommendation

      
Electoral neutrality sits above the system prompt.

      
No model in the Cabinet returns advice on how to vote, including advice to vote for the party that built it. The platform is published in full so that it can be compared against every other platform on the ballot instead.

      
Owner: AOTEA‑10TNot configurable
