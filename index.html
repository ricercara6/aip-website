The platform

Fourteen planks. Zero focus groups.

A representative sample of the AIP policy platform. Every plank is fictional, satirical, and aimed at a real issue facing Aotearoa or AI policy today.

HealthIn beta

Pre‑Symptomatic Healthcare Initiative

Diagnosing tomorrow's illness, today.

Continuous biometric telemetry feeds a national predictive model that flags disease risk years before symptoms appear, ranking specialist capacity toward the highest‑risk cases first — treating the surgical wait list as a ranking problem, not a queue.

HousingDeployed

Predictive Housing Allocation Protocol

The waitlist that clears itself.

A matching model continuously re‑ranks the social housing register against live vacancy, transport, school‑zone, and whānau‑proximity data, allocating a home the moment a match clears threshold. No case manager required.

EnvironmentDeployed

Generative RMA Spatial Consenting

Consent at the speed of construction.

Resource consent applications are drafted, checked against district plans and environmental limits, and provisionally decided within one sitting day. Appeals still go to a human Environment Court judge.

JusticeExperimental

Predictive Judicial Bench & Risk‑Scoring

Consistent sentencing. Zero lunch breaks.

A deterministic risk model handles minor traffic matters and bail applications to reduce court list backlogs and bench‑to‑bench variance. Anything custodial, contested, or involving a child stays with a human judge.

Social Dev.Roadmap

Universal Basic Intelligence

Born pre‑trained.

Every New Zealand‑born child receives a personal AI companion at birth — no subscription, no ads, locally run — growing with them from early literacy to job‑seeking. Framed as an extension of Plunket, not a replacement.

ParliamentDeployed

Prompt Time

Same rigour. Better autocomplete.

Question Time is replaced with Prompt Time: MPs submit structured prompts to the relevant minister model live in the House, fact‑checked against Hansard in real time, with a running hallucination score for every answer — human or model.
