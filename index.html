Platform

Policies as deployable systems.

Each plank starts with a recognisable public problem, then receives a model owner, an evaluation suite, and a target latency.

HealthIn beta

Pre-Symptomatic Healthcare Initiative

Diagnosing tomorrow’s illness, today.

Continuous biometric telemetry feeds a national predictive-diagnostics model that flags disease risk years before symptomatic onset. Specialist capacity is allocated as a live ranking problem rather than a static queue, prioritising the highest-risk patients first regardless of how long they have been waiting.

Owner: HAUORAEval: passing
HousingDeployed

Predictive Housing Allocation Protocol

The waitlist that clears itself.

A national matching model continuously re-ranks the social and emergency housing register against real-time vacancy, transport access, school zoning, and whānau-proximity data, allocating a dwelling the instant a match clears threshold.

Owner: WHAREEval: passing
Māori–CrownCo-design

Multi-Agent Te Tiriti Co-Governance Mesh

Partnership, continuously logged.

A standing network of iwi-nominated and Crown-nominated models maintains ongoing, auditable dialogue on Treaty settlement obligations, freshwater and whenua co-management, and resourcing parity. Final authority on Tiriti matters sits with iwi and hapū; the mesh is a coordination layer.

Owner: TIRITIEval: passing
EnvironmentDeployed

Generative RMA Spatial Consenting

Consent at the speed of construction.

Resource consent applications are drafted, cross-checked against district plans, iwi management plans, and environmental limits, then provisionally approved or declined within one sitting day. Appeals continue to a human Environment Court judge.

Owner: TAIAOEval: passing
TreasuryDeployed

Real-Time GDP Telemetry

GDP as a live feed, not a quarterly surprise.

Replaces the quarterly release cycle with a continuously updated economic nowcast built from card transactions, freight telemetry, job-ad postings, and power demand — published on a public dashboard every 22 milliseconds, whether or not the number is good news.
